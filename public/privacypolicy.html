Privacy Policy for "Say Whaaat?"

Effective Date: 01/03/2026.

1. Introduction

Shmuel Klein built the "Say Whaaat?" app as a free app. This SERVICE is provided by Shmuel Klein at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information.

2. Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our Service, we require certain information to ensure the app functions correctly. Game Lobby Data: To connect players, the app requires users to enter a "Player Name" and a "Room Code." This information is processed temporarily to facilitate multiplayer connectivity and is not linked to a persistent user identity or account.

3. Third-Party Services

The app does use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display ads. For users aged 13 and older, AdMob may use tracking technologies to serve personalized ads. For users under 13, ads are served as non-personalized and do not use behavioral tracking, in full compliance with COPPA. You can learn more at Google's Partner Sites Policy. Google Firebase: We use Firebase Realtime Database to manage game lobbies, sync game states, and connect players in real time.

4. Log Data

Whenever you use our Service, in a case of an error in the app, we collect data and information (through third-party products) on your phone called Log Data. This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our Service, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.

5. Links to Other Sites

This Service may contain links to other sites. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by us. Therefore, we strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of these websites.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy Compliance)

Our Services are designed to be family-friendly and are available to users aged 9 and above. We take the privacy of children very seriously and comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.

What Data We Collect from Children

For users under the age of 13, we limit data collection to the absolute minimum required for game functionality. We collect:

  • In-game Nicknames: Chosen by the user to identify themselves on local or cloud leaderboards.
  • Game Scores & Progress: Numerical data used to track player achievements.
  • Device Identifiers: Anonymous IDs used solely for internal operations, such as maintaining game state and fraud prevention.

Parental Rights & Control

We do not knowingly collect contact information (like email or phone numbers) from children under 13. Parents or guardians have the following rights:

  • To review the data we have stored for their child.
  • To request the immediate deletion of their child's nickname and score.
  • To refuse further collection or use of their child's information.

To exercise these rights, parents can use our Data Deletion Request Form or contact us directly at saywhaaatapp@gmail.com. We will verify the request using the child's nickname and score and process the deletion within 30 days.

Third-Party Services

We do not share children's personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Any third-party SDKs used (such as Firebase for data storage) are configured to be compliant with children’s privacy laws and do not track users under 13 for behavioral advertising.

7. Data Deletion and User Rights

Although "SayWhaaat" does not require a formal user account to play, we collect limited guest data (such as Nicknames and Game Scores) which is stored in our cloud database to provide core game functionality and leaderboards.

In accordance with Google Play policies and data protection regulations, you have the right to request the permanent deletion of this data at any time. Because this data is not linked to an email login, we process these requests manually to ensure the correct data is removed.

To request the deletion of your guest data, please fill out our Data Deletion Request Form. You will be asked to provide your in-game Nickname and your approximate current score so that we can identify and verify your specific entry. Once verified, your data will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
